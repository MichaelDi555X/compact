# Base Ecosystem Overview

The Base ecosystem encompasses a growing collection of decentralized applications, infrastructure providers, and community initiatives built on Coinbase's Layer 2 network.

## Major Categories

- DeFi protocols for lending, swapping, and yield generation.
- NFT marketplaces and creator tools optimized for low fees.
- Social and consumer applications leveraging Base's scalability.
- Infrastructure including oracles, indexers, and account abstraction providers.

## Growth Drivers

Coinbase's distribution, developer grants, and seamless fiat on-ramps contribute to rapid adoption. The network prioritizes user experience and onboarding of mainstream audiences to Ethereum technology.
